# Design and analysis of a pinch roll

**BSc thesis — Politecnico di Milano — 2024**

## Objective

Design and analyse a pinch-roll mechanism, taking it from concept through to
engineering validation.

## Method

- **CAD modelling** in **SolidWorks**.
- Mechanical and gear analysis in **KISSsoft**, from geometry to load and
  durability checks.

## Relevance to vehicle aerodynamics

Mechanical-design breadth: the CAD-to-analysis discipline behind packaging,
mounting and manufacturability of aero components, complementing the CFD work.

## Tools

`SolidWorks` · `KISSsoft`
