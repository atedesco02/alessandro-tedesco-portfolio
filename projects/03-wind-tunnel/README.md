# Experimental fluid dynamics — wind-tunnel campaign

**Academic project — Politecnico di Milano — 2025/2026**
Course 091253 (Prof. A. Zanotti). Group work (4 authors). Full report in Italian
below; English summary here.

## Summary

A campaign of three wind-tunnel laboratories at the DAER teaching tunnel and the
De Ponte closed-circuit tunnel, covering force, pressure, hot-wire and optical
(PIV) measurement techniques on aerodynamic bodies — including a Formula 1 rear
wing.

## Lab 1 — Force measurements

Calibration of a three-component strain-gauge balance (FUTEK biaxial + torsional
load cells, Wheatstone bridge, LabVIEW acquisition) and of the wind tunnel, then
measurement of the lift, drag and moment polars (CL, CD, CM) of a finite
**NACA0015** wing.

## Lab 2 — Pressure and hot-wire

Static-pressure distribution over a **NACA23012** profile using 16 pressure taps,
integrated to obtain the pressure coefficient and the CL–α curve; followed by a
**hot-wire anemometry** wake survey to compute profile drag from the momentum
deficit, with frequency/spectral analysis and turbulence-intensity estimation.

## Lab 3 — Particle Image Velocimetry

Two-component **PIV** characterisation of the wake of a **Formula 1 rear wing**
(2021 regulations) in the De Ponte tunnel: seeding (Laskin nozzle, DEHS),
dual-pulse laser sheet, dual high-resolution CMOS cameras, calibration, image
acquisition and vector-field reconstruction (PIVTEC / SigMaX).

## Techniques

`Wind-tunnel testing` · `Strain-gauge force balance` · `Pressure taps / Cp integration`
· `Hot-wire anemometry` · `PIV (2C)` · `LabVIEW acquisition` · `Signal & spectral analysis`

## Relevance to vehicle aerodynamics

Directly aligned with how aero teams work: wind-tunnel force and pressure
measurement and PIV are the backbone of experimental aero development and of
CFD-to-tunnel correlation. Lab 3 applies these to an actual F1 aero component.
